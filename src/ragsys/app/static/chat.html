          About & sources

          A study assistant for students and developers. Each message is answered by one language model call; passages from the selected documents are added automatically when they match the question, and the reply cites them. Verify checks a cited reply with deterministic citation and number guards and a critic model. Grounded only answers strictly from the documents or says they do not cover the question.

          Non-commercial use. Several document collections are licensed for non-commercial use only (for example CC BY-NC-SA and CC BY-NC); this demo is free and non-commercial. Excerpts shown remain under their original licences listed below.

          Answers can be wrong. Law collections: not legal advice. Human biology: educational, not medical advice; for symptoms or emergencies contact a doctor or local emergency services.
