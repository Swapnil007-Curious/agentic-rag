            About & sources

            Veridex is a knowledge assistant for students and developers. Your question is embedded and the documents are searched in your browser; the passages that match are sent with the question and the answer cites them. Each message is answered by one language model call through a small rate-limited proxy. Verify checks a cited answer with citation and number guards (in the browser) and a critic model. Grounded only answers strictly from the documents or says they do not cover the question.

            Privacy. Your questions, the recent conversation and the passages selected for a question go to the proxy and the model provider (Groq) to produce the answer; neither stores message contents. Uploaded PDFs never leave your browser except as passages included in a question. Chats are stored only in this browser.

            Non-commercial use. Several document collections are licensed for non-commercial use only (for example CC BY-NC-SA and CC BY-NC); this site is free and non-commercial. Excerpts shown remain under their original licences listed below.

            Answers can be wrong. Law collections: not legal advice. Human biology: educational, not medical advice; for symptoms or emergencies contact a doctor or local emergency services.
